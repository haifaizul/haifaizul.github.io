01 THE PHILOMATH

FH B. 2007.09.04 · SERENDIP

I'm Faizul Hai — born on 04.09.2007 in Serendip, the old name for Sri Lanka that quietly slipped into English as the word serendipity. Fitting, honestly: I keep accidentally finding things worth learning.

I'm in the A/L 2026 batch, currently studying Mathematics, Physics and Chemistry — and I treat the syllabus as a floor, not a ceiling. I'm a philomath by instinct: I collect fields the way other people collect stamps, and the long game is a slow, happy transition from philomath to polymath.

When the textbooks close, I build. Physics simulators to watch mechanics move, small web tools, note apps, silly games — all of it published on GitHub, most of it born out of pure curiosity.
